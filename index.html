WORDWISE QUEST

THE GRADE 6 READING ADVENTURE

READ • RECOGNIZE • PRONOUNCE • ACHIEVE

Hi, explorer! Read sight words, earn stars and unlock badges. Ready for the quest?

How to play

  1. Pick a level, then a batch of 10 words.
  2. Press PLAY WORD to hear it.
  3. Press the microphone and read the word aloud.
  4. You get 2 tries. Any result lets you move to the next word.
  5. Finish 10 words to see your report and stars.

Needs: Chrome or Edge, internet, and microphone permission. No voice recordings are saved. If your browser can't listen, your teacher can check your reading.
